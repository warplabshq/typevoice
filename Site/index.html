FAQ

Questions people ask.

Why does it need Accessibility and Microphone?

Microphone, to hear you while you hold the key. Accessibility, to notice the key anywhere on the Mac and to paste the text into the app you're using. It never reads your screen.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the one-time model download on first launch, dictation needs no connection at all.

Which languages?

English, all accents. The model is English-only by design; that's what makes it this accurate and this fast.

What happens after the trial?

Dictation pauses until you buy it, once, on the App Store. Everything you dictated stays in Summary.

Which Macs?

Any Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.
