Privacy Policy

Last updated · Applies to the Murmur app for macOS and this website.

Murmur is built so that we know nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. Your voice and your text stay on your Mac. This policy explains the few places where data moves at all.

1. What the app processes, and where

DataWhere it is processedWhere it is stored
Your voice while you hold the triggerOn your Mac, by a speech model running on the Neural EngineNot stored. Discarded as soon as it is transcribed, unless you turn on Voice notes (see 3)
The transcribed textOn your Mac. Optional cleanup uses Apple Intelligence's on-device modelOn your Mac, in the app's History (an SQLite file) until you delete it
Your Dictionary and settingsOn your MacOn your Mac, as plain files

Nothing above is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we do not want to.

2. Network requests the app makes

  • Speech model download. On first launch the app downloads its speech-recognition model (about 450 MB) from Hugging Face's public servers. As with any download, their servers see your IP address. No personal data is sent. Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • App Store purchases. Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple's App Store. To validate a purchase across your Macs, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the purchase receipt from Apple. It does not receive your name, email, voice or text. RevenueCat privacy policy.

That is the complete list. The app does not contact our servers, because we do not run any.

3. Voice notes (optional)

If you turn on Settings › Voice notes › Keep recordings, each dictation is also saved as a small audio file on your Mac so you can drag it into a message. The files stay on your Mac and are deleted when you delete the dictation, turn the setting off and press Delete Recordings, or use Delete Everything.

4. Permissions

The app asks macOS for Microphone access to hear you, and Accessibility access to notice the trigger key anywhere on the Mac and to paste the finished text into the app you are using. It does not read screen contents, keystrokes you type, or anything in other apps.

5. This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security, typically for a short period.

6. Email

If you write to support, we keep the conversation for as long as needed to help you, then delete it. We do not add you to any list.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children; we do not collect data from anyone.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on our side. Everything the app stores is on your Mac under your control: Privacy › Reveal in Finder shows the files, and Delete Everything removes them. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California or elsewhere with data-protection rights, you can still contact us with any question and we will answer.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
