Privacy Policy

Last updated · Applies to the Murmur app for macOS and this website.

Murmur is built so that we know nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no server of ours. Your voice and your text stay on your Mac. This policy explains the few places where any data moves at all.

1. What the app processes, and where

DataWhere it is processedWhere it is stored
Your voice while you hold the triggerOn your Mac, by an open speech model (NVIDIA Parakeet) running on the Neural EngineNot stored. Discarded as soon as it is transcribed, unless you turn on the audio option (see 3)
The transcribed textOn your Mac. Optional cleanup uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model, when it is available on your MacOn your Mac, in the app's Summary (an SQLite database) until you delete it
Your Dictionary and settingsOn your MacOn your Mac, as a JSON file and standard app preferences
An optional debug logOff by default. Written on your Mac only if you turn it onOn your Mac, in the app's Logs folder

Nothing above is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we do not want to.

2. Network requests the app makes

  • Speech model download. On first launch the app downloads its speech-recognition model (about 450 MB) from Hugging Face's public servers. As with any download, their servers see your IP address. No personal data is sent, and the download is not repeated. Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • Purchase checks. The free trial and the purchase are handled by Apple's App Store. To know whether this Mac has a valid purchase, the app checks with RevenueCat when it launches and when you buy or restore. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous identifier the app makes up for this purpose, the App Store receipt, your IP address, and basic device information such as the macOS and app versions. It does not receive your name, your email, your voice or your text. RevenueCat privacy policy.

That is the complete list. Dictation itself never needs a connection.

3. Audio after each dictation (optional)

If you turn on Settings › Indicator › Offer the audio after each dictation, each dictation is also saved on your Mac as a small AAC file, and a Drag audio chip appears on the pill. Dragging the chip, or a row in Summary, into another app hands that app a copy of the file; from then on it is subject to that app's privacy policy. The files stay on your Mac and are deleted when you delete the dictation, press Delete Recordings in Privacy, or use Delete Everything.

4. Permissions and the clipboard

The app asks macOS for Microphone access to hear you, and Accessibility access so it can notice your trigger key anywhere on the Mac and paste the finished text into the app you are using. The Accessibility permission lets the app see key presses; it acts only on the trigger key, and on Escape while dictating, and records nothing else. It does not read the contents of your screen or of other apps.

To type the text, the app places it on the clipboard, pastes it, and then restores whatever was on the clipboard before, within about half a second. It does not keep or transmit what was on your clipboard.

5. This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties; its fonts are served from this site. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security, typically for a short period.

6. Email

If you write to support, we keep the conversation for as long as needed to help you, then delete it. We do not add you to any list.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children; we do not collect data from anyone.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on our side. Everything the app stores is on your Mac under your control: Privacy › Reveal in Finder shows the files, Summary can export them, and Delete Everything removes them. RevenueCat's records are tied only to the anonymous identifier described above; if you want them removed, write to us with the identifier shown in the app's License tab and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California or elsewhere with data-protection rights, you can contact us with any question and we will answer.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
