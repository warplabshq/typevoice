Support

We answer within a day or two. Email support.

It asks for Microphone and Accessibility. Why?

Microphone, to hear you while you hold the key. Accessibility, to notice the key you hold anywhere on the Mac and to paste the finished text into the app you're using. Murmur never reads what is on your screen. Both switches live in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The 🌐 key opens the emoji picker.

System Settings › Keyboard › "Press 🌐 key to" → Do Nothing. Or pick another trigger under Settings › Dictating: Right ⌘, Right ⌥, or a combination like ⌥Space.

Accessibility looks on but nothing happens.

Flip the Murmur switch off and on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, then quit and reopen the app. The menu bar shows a fix-it item whenever the permission is missing.

Nothing was typed, but the text is in Summary.

Some places can't take pasted text (the desktop, a button). The pill offers a Copy button in that case, and every dictation is kept in Summary where you can copy it later.

Where is my data?

Privacy › Reveal in Finder opens the folder. History is an SQLite file, the dictionary is JSON, and voice notes (if you turned them on) are small AAC files. Delete Everything removes all of it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the one-time model download on first launch, dictation needs no connection at all.

Which languages?

English, all accents. The recognition model is English-only by design; it is what makes it this accurate and this fast.

Refunds and purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Restore Purchases in the License tab brings a purchase to another Mac signed in to the same Apple ID.

Keyboard cheatsheet

Hold the trigger to dictate. Tap it twice to keep listening hands-free. esc cancels. Say "new line", "new paragraph", "bullet …" or "number one …, number two …" to shape the text.
